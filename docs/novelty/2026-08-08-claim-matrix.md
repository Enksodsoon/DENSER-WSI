# MC-V2 Research-Novelty Claim Matrix

Status: **provisional; novelty and breakthrough are not established**.

The research hypothesis is narrower than adaptive or pathology-aware compression:
MC-V2 combines compact evidence-contract allocation, decoder-verifiable evidence
certificates, deterministic sparse repair, complete-byte selection, and independently
decodable fail-closed WSI tiles.

| Prior work | Known overlap | Required MC-V2 differentiation |
|---|---|---|
| [AdaSlide](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41339637/) | Region-dependent pathology compression and restoration | Self-verifying contract, stored repair bytes, matched complete-byte comparator, independent tile failure semantics |
| [Feature-preserving WSI compression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.11350) | Pathology-feature similarity objective | Decoder-verifiable evidence payload and deterministic non-neural repair/fallback |
| [Task-driven region-adaptive JPEG](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.patrec.2022.11.020) | Block-wise task-aware quality allocation | Physical-scale H&E contract, WSI container, certificate, repair, and full-slide inference |
| [WISE](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Mao_WISE_A_Framework_for_Gigapixel_Whole-Slide-Image_Lossless_Compression_CVPR_2025_paper.html) | Gigapixel WSI compression framework | Lossy evidence-contract allocation with verified repair and matched standard portfolio |
| JPEG XL / JPEG 2000 | Adaptive transforms, ROI coding, random access building blocks | Evidence-specific allocation and acceptance are outside the standards themselves |

Before any public novelty statement, this matrix must be expanded with standards clauses,
forward/backward citations, and patent claim charts. A positive technical result permits only
a dated “to our knowledge” research statement, never a patentability or legal world-first claim.
