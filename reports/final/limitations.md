# Limitations

- The storage-feasible reduced cohort was not processed because the measured full-grid method runtime was multi-day under the frozen six-worker cap.
- Only one development-partition source was downloaded and checksum-verified for eligibility and throughput calibration.
- No pilot, tuning, freeze, or final compression outcome was observed; no compression-performance inference is available.
- Four host-only native-codec integration checks were structurally skipped; all pinned codecs were available in the container benchmark.
